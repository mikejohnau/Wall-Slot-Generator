How to use

Workflow

  1. In the Design workspace, open Add-Ins and click Wall Slot Generator
  2. An active monthly or yearly subscription is required. Choose Subscribe, complete payment on the Autodesk App Store, then choose Check again
  3. Enter your wall dimensions — width, height, thickness and border width
  4. Choose a pattern type and adjust the slot spacing slider
  5. Check the live preview and calculated layout
  6. Click Generate wall — a component named Wall Slot Panel is created
  7. To iterate: change any value and click Generate again — the previous wall is replaced automatically

Pattern types

Design 1 — Rows + inter-rows
Large stadium slots in full-width rows, with a [small · large · small] triplet row nestled between each pair. Maximum slot count. Best for tool walls and display panels.

Design 2 — Brick offset
Rows of identical slots offset by half a pitch on alternate rows. Optional half-slots (one flat end) fill the ends of offset rows. Best for cabinet backs and architectural screening.

Slot spacing slider

Drag left for larger gaps and fewer slots (decorative / open feel), right for tighter packing and more slots. Loose (≈57%) matches classic slatwall proportions. Tight (≈86%) gives maximum hook density.

Border width

Sets the gap from the wall edge to the start of the slot field on all four sides. Set to 0 for no border. A 20–30mm border makes a sturdy mounting flange for screw-fixing to a wall or cabinet.

Multiple panels in one design

Generate a panel, then rename the component in the browser (right-click → Rename) before you click Wall Slot Generator and generate again. Generate only replaces the component named Wall Slot Panel — renamed components are left untouched.

After generating

  • CAM: Switch to Manufacture workspace — slot profiles work directly with 2D Pocket and 2D Contour operations
  • 3D print: Export as STL or 3MF via File → Export
  • Laser / CNC: Export DXF from the sketch or Drawings workspace
  • Assembly: Use Joint or As-Built Joint to position the panel in a larger design
  • Material: Right-click the body → Appearance to assign timber, MDF, aluminium, etc.
Privacy policy

Publisher: Michael, M2 Design
Effective date: 24 September 2026

1. Data collected and how it is used

Wall Slot Generator stores your last-used panel settings (wall dimensions, design type, packing density, half-ends toggle) as Fusion 360 document attributes on your local machine. Those values only pre-populate the panel the next time you open it in the same document.

Once each Fusion session, the add-in sends your Autodesk user id and this app's id to Autodesk (apps.autodesk.com) to check that your monthly or yearly subscription is active. A successful check is cached on this computer for up to 7 days so generation can continue offline. No wall dimensions or design data are sent.

2. Third parties

The subscription check is sent only to Autodesk's App Store entitlement service. This add-in has no analytics, advertising, or other third-party services.

3. Data retention and deletion

Preferences persist as Fusion 360 document attributes for as long as the document exists. They are removed when you delete the document, or by uninstalling the add-in.

4. Revoking consent and requesting deletion

No data is held remotely. To remove all locally stored data: delete or close the Fusion 360 document, or remove the wall_slots folder from your Fusion 360 AddIns directory.
